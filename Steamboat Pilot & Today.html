Picture book by former Steamboat Springs Winter Sports Club racer honors Howelsen Hill

All proceeds to benefit the club; free reading planned for Dec. 5 at Off the Beaten Path Bookstore

Local author and former Steamboat Springs Winter Sports Club racer Heidi Hillenbrand Wehrly has published “The Fastest Feeling,” a new picture book illustrated by Sloane Speer that honors the role of Howelsen Hill for generations of Steamboat children, with all proceeds benefiting the SSWSC, according to a recent statement.

In the book, Hazel, a speed-loving young bear, tries several SSWSC disciplines at Howelsen Hill, settles on Alpine racing and finishes fourth in the big night race; support, not a podium, closes the story.

Wehrly wrote it for kids who try hard without winning and to give back to the club; SSWSC Executive Director Brian Krill thanked both creators.

Speer, a former SSWSC kid skiing Howelsen since age 5, illustrated the lodge, chairlift and night lights.

The free reading is 10:30 a.m. Saturday, Dec. 5, at Off the Beaten Path Bookstore, with cookies, cocoa and signed copies.

The book, for ages 3-8, sells there, at SSWSC events, online and at TheFastestFeeling.com.
