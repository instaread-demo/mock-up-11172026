Though the domestic Arizona Fall League gets most of the attention this time of year (be sure to check out our weekly AFL Hot Sheet rankings), winter ball seasons the Dominican Republic, Mexico and Venezuela are also now underway.

For 2026, there are a number of Top 100 Prospects taking part across the various leagues, including , Dodgers shortstop Emil Morales, Giants shortstop Jhonny Level, Reds catcher Alfredo Duno, Rockies outfielder Roldy Brito, Mets shortstop Jefferson Rojas and, most notably, No. 1 overall prospect Jesús Made of the Brewers.

Winter ball gives affiliated players a way to keep competing through the offseason, and the action this year stretches across three circuits: the Dominican league, the Mexican Pacific league and the Venezuelan league.

The prospects named above are among the players suiting up this winter, with Made, the top-ranked prospect in the game, the most notable name in the group.

Baseball America is tracking every MLB-affiliated player taking part in these leagues and will refresh the list each week for as long as the winter seasons run, so check back regularly for the latest updates.
