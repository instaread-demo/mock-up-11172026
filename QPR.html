MIDDLESBROUGH await QPR first in this season’s FA Youth Cup.

It follows Friday’s third round draw, which saw Rangers enter the hat alongside clubs from the Premier League and Championship.

Tom Charles’ Under-18s – who bowed out to Brighton & Hove Albion in the fourth round last year – were drawn at home against Boro in one of 32 ties.

A date for our Third Round contest will be confirmed in due course, with all ties required to be played by Saturday 12th December.
