Harambee Stars vs Bafana Bafana: A social media match kicks off

Kenyan and South African fans have been raring to throng Nyayo National Stadium in Nairobi to watch the clash of their national teams. But, long before the actual match scheduled for Tuesday evening, the spectators had already launched their duel, online.

“Thank you wena. Throw those stones without ceasing. We’ll need them to build a new airport,” a Kenyan fan Oyofrah Francis replied to South African supporters after they began making fun of the potholes visible in footage of Bafana Bafana arriving in Nairobi. He tagged Kenyan President William Ruto for good measure.

That exchange is one of many that have filled timelines on both sides, with supporters trading jokes, memes and friendly jabs in the build-up to the AFCON Group D qualifier at Nyayo National Stadium.

Rivalries like this now routinely play out on social media long before kick-off. For many fans, the online back-and-forth has become part of the matchday experience, turning the days before a game into a contest of their own.

Both squads have been preparing in separate training sessions ahead of Tuesday evening's meeting in Nairobi.

Once the match gets under way, attention will move from the timelines to the pitch, where the result will settle the argument, at least until the two sides meet again.
