Flight canceled, rental cars unavailable: Man rents U-Haul to drive 1,200 miles from Texas to Minnesota

After struggling to find a flight or rental car after widespread air travel disruptions, Greg Swan turned to an unconventional solution to get home to his family.

A canceled flight and a shortage of rental cars left one traveler with an unusual way to get home to Minnesota from Texas: a 10-foot U-Haul truck.

Greg Swan was visiting Austin, Texas, from Minneapolis, Minnesota, for a conference when widespread flight disruptions left him scrambling to get home.

After the Federal Aviation Administration issued a nationwide ground stop Friday, Swan's flight was rebooked for Saturday. But about 3 a.m. Saturday, he received another cancellation notice.

Swan had to be home by Monday for his kids as his wife began a new job, but he found no flights or cars along I-35. An AI tool proposed a U-Haul, so he and a co-worker left South Austin early Saturday for a six-state drive.

Swan called the truck noisy and slow, with no cruise control. Stopping only for fuel and short walks, the pair spent 21 hours on the road, and Swan got home at 4 a.m. Sunday.

His U-Haul post on X topped 4 million views by Monday and drew "Home Alone" polka band memes. Swan said he was glad to be home for his family and "pretty dang happy" with it.

The rental cost about $1,200, which he called manageable after his refund and a split with his colleague. The truck was due back by Thursday.
