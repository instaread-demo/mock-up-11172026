Two more Fed hikes, then a long pause: Goldman reads a third hot core CPI print

From investinglive.com

Goldman Sachs Asset Management expects the Federal Reserve to keep raising interest rates beyond December, after a third consecutive strong reading on US core inflation. It forecasts back-to-back hikes in December and January, followed by a prolonged pause. Core consumer prices, which exclude food and energy, rose 0.3% in October. That is an annualized pace of about 3.6% and was above market expectations. Goldman said the run of three firm prints suggests the softer readings over the summer were temporary rather than the start of a sustained slowdown in inflation. The bank's accompanying charts also track how core prices have moved on a three-month annualized basis. (full story)
