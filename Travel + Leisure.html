I live for solo trips. I think they’re one of the best ways to travel, see the world, and connect with yourself and others—even if you’re an introvert. You’re able to be on your own time, do the things that you truly want to do, and really be in the moment. I’ve been on at least 35 solo trips in my lifetime, and I imagine that there will be many more to come. However, when you travel solo, especially as a woman, you must take the necessary precautions.

Going it alone, at home or overseas, can mean risky moments. Research, discretion about traveling solo and smart safety gear help, but some walks after dark are unavoidable.

Below are the purse essentials carried for those nights, all sold on Amazon, several discounted before Black Friday.
