Celtics-Magic recap: C's overwhelmed in fourth, snapping win streak at six

The Celtics ran out of gas on the second night of a back-to-back.

By Michael Hurley and Chris Forsberg, Celtics Insider •
Published November 16, 2026 • Updated on November 16, 2026 at 11:43 pm

What to Know

  • Orlando won 115-105 in Boston, taking the fourth quarter by a 40-28 margin.
  • Boston's run of victories ends at six straight.
  • Desmond Bane topped a balanced Magic attack with 24 points, with Franz Wagner adding 21 and Anthony Black 20.
  • For the Celtics, Jayson Tatum scored 24, Paul George 22 and Payton Pritchard 21.

The Celtics trailed by 9 points at halftime to the Magic on Monday but stormed out of halftime and held a lead at the end of the third quarter.

As it turned out, though, that third quarter was essentially their last gasp, and the well-rested Magic were simply relentless over the final 12 minutes, ultimately beating the Celtics on their home court, 115-105.

The Celtics just never got into a rhythm during this game, and the Magic made it hurt.

Check out the live blog below for the full recap of all that happened at TD Garden.

Boston nursed a two-point edge into the final period, then Orlando answered with a 37-23 run for a 10-point win.

Jayson Tatum downplays effects of hand wrap to start game

By Michael Hurley

Tatum started cold while wearing a left-hand wrap he later removed. He said it only eased swelling from a bruise and denied it was a factor.

Joe Mazzulla: Celtics trusting Chris Cenac Jr. ‘more and more'
