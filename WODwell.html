Every athlete and fitness coach knows the feeling of being stuck and lacking the ‘fire’. The feeling of pulling up your favorite source of workout inspiration… and you just keep scrolling. The right workout doesn’t show itself; the workouts use equipment you don’t have. Or every workout includes that one movement you’ve been doing all week (burpees anyone?).

Meanwhile the clock keeps ticking, so you settle for whatever comes up next and grind through it without much enthusiasm. Coaches writing sessions for clients feel that letdown even more.

Find the needle in the haystack without picking through the straw.

Users said they lose time sifting through thousands of WODs; the fix is better filtering, and these updates grew out of that feedback.

Complete control begins with seeing only what you want

Filter boxes used to start checked, so you had to clear them all first. Now they start empty, and VAULT members get their extra categories included automatically.

Filter in, Filter out

Gyms close, sessions move home and coaches plan for single athletes, so searches can now be narrowed much further.

Bodyweight only

Switching off the equipment toggle shows only workouts that need no gear.

Include and/or Exclude Movements

You can also keep or drop movements, modalities and body parts.

Use case: coach Jenny pulls existing WODs that fit each athlete’s movement needs and saves hours of programming a week.

Include and/or Exclude Equipment

Few people own every machine, so results can be limited to gear you have or exclude gear you lack.

Garage athlete Scott, with dumbbells, a rower and a jump rope, now finds a fitting WOD far faster.

Basic filters and custom collections are free; paid VAULT members get advanced filters.

Advanced Filters: Focus Areas/Body Parts

Target upper body, lower body or core, or pulling, pressing and squatting, so someone who did heavy legs yesterday can find upper-body work.

Advanced Filters: Modalities

Isolate Weightlifting, Gymnastics or Cardio, for example strength work for a football player.
