Nami’s Tips for Holiday Dinner Prep

A little planning makes hosting much calmer.

  1. Plan your menu early. Balance dishes you can make ahead with quick ones for the day itself.
  2. Prep ingredients ahead. Get chopping, measuring and sauces done a day or two in advance.
  3. Cook in stages. Make soups, braises and sauces early to avoid a last-minute rush.
  4. Set the table early. Lay out tableware and decor the night before.
  5. Share the workload. Let guests contribute a dish or drink and have family pitch in.
