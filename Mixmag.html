New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani appoints new nightlife director

Eric Thor has been made Director of the Office of Nightlife, aiming to support the industry from the “DJs and bartenders to security staff”

New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani has appointed a brand new nightlife director, to support the “workers, small businesses and communities that make New York’s nightlife possible”.

Mamdani announced the appointment of Eric Thor as Executive Director of the Office of Nightlife on Friday, November 13, confirming that he will soon begin meeting with workers, business owners, and community members.

Thor will meet with industry affiliates across all five boroughs of New York to “help shape the office’s priorities”, with an aim to improve conditions and licensing stipulations for nightlife workers.

Mamdani framed an affordable evening out as central to city life, and nightlife as a driver of jobs and small businesses held back by red tape.

He also pointed to Thor’s past role as lead developer at LaborLab, a group that helps workplaces unionise.

The Mayor’s Office describes the Queens-born Thor as a longtime, active figure in the city’s nightlife.

Thor wants nights out to be affordable, nightlife jobs safe and fair, and new venues easier to open without costly lawyers.
