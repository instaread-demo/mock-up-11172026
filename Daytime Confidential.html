Jada and Shawn are strolling through Horton Square after their dinner. They get all adorable when he says he has something special planned for later. Just then, Jada gets a frantic call from Lani and says they will be right there.

Sarah walks into Xander’s room after having been summoned. He tells her about the gun and its attachment to a prior murder. Xander promises he knew nothing about any such crime and all but begs her to believe him. Sarah is non-responsive and then gets notified she is needed elsewhere. With that, she exits.

Friends and Loved Ones Arrive to Check on Leo

In the exam room, Sarah takes charge of Leo’s care while Javi pleads with him to pull through, then she phones Chad, who is listed as his emergency contact. A tearful call from Javi sends Gabi and Gwen racing over from their pizza night.

Once Jada and Shawn reach the hospital, Javi explains that Leo had been digging into EJ and counterfeit goods at DiMera Enterprises for The Spectator, and he is sure EJ is behind the attack. Jada promises a thorough investigation.

Gabi and Chad Reconnect on Days of Our Lives

Sarah reports that Leo is stable and sends Javi back to him, while Gwen updates Steve and mentions the warehouse, so Jada sends Shawn to the piers. Gabi and Chad share their worry and guilt over Leo’s risky reporting, hug, and agree EJ is the likely culprit; Chad vows to get the story into The Spectator with backing from Theo and Johnny, and the two pledge to support each other.

Javi’s Love Helps Leo Regain Consciousness

Lying beside Leo, Javi tells him how much he loves and needs him, and Leo wakes, asks what happened and calls Javi his hero. He tells Jada a goon struck his head and smothered him until he blacked out, and that he came to in the river; Javi worries about a concussion after so many recent head injuries.

Gwen scolds Leo for his recklessness, then softens and urges him to marry Javi again. Chad praises and teases his friend and vows to bring down his brother himself, and Sarah says Leo must stay a few days and, due to budget cuts, share a room.

Previous Days of Our Lives (DAYS) recap: Leo’s Fate Hangs in The Balance

Sarah tells Xander she believes him and wants to face the fight at his side, until Gwen interrupts and is stunned to learn Xander is the one delaying a reunion. Gabi overhears the detectives discussing Leo’s tip about the warehouse, fake purses and cash.

Later, Xander and Leo end up as roommates, Gwen tells Sarah she and Xander belong together, Chad confides in Gabi about strain with Belle, and Jada and Shawn find the crate that held Leo empty.
